    FIELD GUIDE

    A small gap. A precise jump.

    1. Reach the right-hand ledge. Get close to the stone plinth. Press Space from a standstill, then briefly tap W once airborne. The small forward drift continues after W is released.
    2. Work into the seam. Run and jump into the gap between the stone wall and the gate edge.
    3. Choose your angle. Hold right mouse to make small facing adjustments. Use the same camera view you use in Era.
    4. Jump forward through the gap. Press Space and W together, then continue onto the far side.

    The gate bars are decorative openings. The original model uses a solid collision box, so the route goes beside it.

    About this prototype

    The Night Elf female, her animation tracks, room textures, and collision triangles come from the installed Era build 1.15.9.70003. Gate placement comes from the Classic encounter database. The browser movement controller is an approximation with adjustable settings.

    Use Calibration to compare known successes and near misses. Attempt exports include your settings, positions, facing, and input timeline. They stay on your computer until you choose to share them.

    Gate placement correction

    The initial placement uses Classic database coordinates. Corrections move the whole original gate, including its collider.

    Controller: fixed 240 Hz, stable ground support and separate wall/jump motion. Collider defaults use the Night Elf model width and height; the live terrain collider is unverified. Standing-jump nudge speed defaults to a provisional 1 yd/s. Exact air speed, contact tolerances and server gate placement still need Era measurements.
    LIVE CLIENT MEASUREMENTS

    Compare an Era recording.

    Record a manual jump in your client, then compare its height, reported speed and timing with your browser attempt. The recording stays in this browser.

    Get the movement recorder

    Download the addon. Put its GothikMovementRecorder folder in your Era Interface/AddOns folder, then reload the client. The addon reads movement data while you move manually.

    1. Stand still on the ledge and run /gothikrecord start 10 idle.
    2. Record one jump with /gothikrecord start 10 running. Use standing or wall for those cases.
    3. Run /gothikrecord stop, then /gothikrecord export. Copy the JSON into the box below.

    Era can restrict position data inside instances. The recorder preserves missing values. Without height, we can still inspect reported speed and input timing.
